# Muryoyi

{% if platform == "ios" %}
Soundscape yana iya amfani da kowace murya da aka girka a iPhone ɗinku, ban da muryoyin Siri. Muryoyin *Enhanced* sun fi daɗin ji, amma sai kun fara sauke su. A manhajar Saituna ta iOS, ku je Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices sannan ku zaɓi murya. Kafin iOS 26, ana kiran *Read & Speak* da *Spoken Content*. Don zaɓar murya a cikin Soundscape, ku buɗe menu sannan ku zaɓi *Saitunan*, sai *Sauti*, sai *Muryoyi*.
{% else %}
Soundscape yana iya amfani da kowace murya da aka girka a wayarku. Don ƙara muryoyi, ku buɗe saitunan wayarku sannan ku nemi *Fitar da rubutu zuwa magana*. Don zaɓar murya a cikin Soundscape, ku buɗe menu sannan ku zaɓi *Saitunan*, sai *Sauti*, sai *Muryoyi*.
{% endif %}
