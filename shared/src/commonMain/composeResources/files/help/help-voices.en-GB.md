# Voices

{% if platform == "ios" %}
Soundscape can use any voice installed on your iPhone, except the Siri voices. The *Enhanced* voices sound best, but you must download them first. In the iOS Settings app, go to Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices and select a voice. Before iOS 26, *Read & Speak* is called *Spoken Content*. To choose a voice in Soundscape, open the menu and select *Settings*, then *Audio*, then *Voices*.
{% else %}
Soundscape can use any voice installed on your phone. To add voices, open your phone's settings and find *Text-to-speech output*. To choose a voice in Soundscape, open the menu and select *Settings*, then *Audio*, then *Voices*.
{% endif %}
